Privacy Policy - Arcade hub

Developer: K@rthikplayz

Effective Date: 26 October 2026

1. About Arcade hub

Arcade hub is an offline gaming application containing multiple single-player and local multiplayer games. Friends and family can play together on one device. The app does not require account creation or login.

2. Information Collection

The app is designed to operate offline. Provided that the published version contains no data-collecting third-party services, it does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or external servers.

3. Local Multiplayer

Players can play together on the same device. Local multiplayer does not by itself require online accounts or internet access.

4. Locally Stored Data

The app may store scores, settings or game progress locally, depending on its features. Users may be able to remove this data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

The app is intended to work offline. If advertising, analytics or other third-party services are included, this policy must be updated to describe their data practices.

6. Data Sharing

Under the offline-only configuration described above, the developer does not receive or share personal information collected through the app.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the offline-only version.

8. Data Security and Retention

The developer does not maintain a server-side database of users' personal information for the offline-only version described in this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.
